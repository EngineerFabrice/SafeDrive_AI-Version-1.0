1. Introduction

SafeDrive AI ("SafeDrive", "the service") is a driver-safety and assistance platform. It uses a driver-facing camera to estimate whether a driver shows visual patterns that may be associated with alcohol-related impairment and, when a driver needs help, connects them with an Umusare — a support driver who belongs to a SafeDrive cooperative — who can drive them to their destination.

SafeDrive AI was created and developed by Fabrice NDAYISABA. These Terms govern your use of the SafeDrive AI website and services. By creating an account or using the service you agree to these Terms and to the Privacy Policy.

You may use SafeDrive only if you are legally able to enter into this agreement, and, if you drive, you hold the licences and permissions the law requires for the vehicle you drive. Drivers and Umusare register through a cooperative participating in SafeDrive. Cooperative managers and administrators are appointed; they cannot self-register.

You are responsible for making sure that your use of SafeDrive complies with the laws and regulations that apply to you.

Membership of a cooperative does not by itself make you verified, and a cooperative that is suspended may stop its members from using some features.

6. Manager verification

Email verification and manager verification are different things and are shown separately:

  • Email verification means that the user controls the email address on the account.
  • Manager (cooperative) verification means that the user's cooperative has reviewed and approved the relevant account information — for drivers this includes the vehicle plate number.

A manager may verify, reject, request more information about, or suspend a member. Rejection or suspension is shown to the member together with the reason the manager provided and the manager's contact details.

7. Limitations of SafeDrive AI

SafeDrive AI is an assistance and safety tool, not a test. It:

  • estimates visual patterns that may be associated with alcohol-related impairment;
  • does not measure blood alcohol concentration (BAC) and never reports a BAC value;
  • does not medically diagnose intoxication or any other condition;
  • does not guarantee that a driver is safe or fit to drive;
  • can produce uncertain or incorrect results, for example because of lighting, camera position, face visibility or the limits of its training data;
  • does not replace legal driving requirements, roadside tests by the authorities, professional judgement or applicable regulations.

Results are expressed only as SOBER, UNCERTAIN or POTENTIALLY NOT SOBER. "SOBER" means only that the system did not detect the patterns it looks for at that moment; "UNCERTAIN" means it could not reliably assess; "POTENTIALLY NOT SOBER" means it detected patterns that may be associated with impairment. No result is a certification of sobriety or a finding of intoxication, and SafeDrive does not allow managers or administrators to change an AI result.

8. Driver responsibility

You remain solely responsible for deciding whether you are fit and legally permitted to drive. Never continue driving because SafeDrive reports "SOBER" if you feel, or have reason to believe, that you are not safe to drive. If you are in doubt, do not drive: request an Umusare, use other transport, or contact someone you trust. In an emergency, contact the local emergency services.

9. Umusare assistance

  • Requests. A driver may request assistance from the safety alert or manually at any time. A manual request never changes the AI result.
  • Matching. SafeDrive offers the request to nearby Umusare who are verified, available and recently located, widening the search if needed. Availability of an Umusare is not guaranteed.
  • Acceptance. The first Umusare to accept is assigned. The driver then sees the Umusare's identity details held by SafeDrive, and both see each other's live location until the request ends.
  • Journey. The Umusare marks arrival and completes the journey at the destination. SafeDrive measures the distance from the Umusare's location updates.
  • Fare. SafeDrive calculates an estimated fare during the journey and a final fare from the measured distance and the pricing in force. Fares are calculated by SafeDrive and cannot be set by either party.
  • Payment confirmation. The driver marks the payment as sent and the Umusare confirms receipt. A payment is shown as completed only after the Umusare confirms it; SafeDrive never treats a payment as completed merely because a driver says they sent it.

Umusare must provide assistance safely, lawfully and only when fit to drive.

10. Location services

  • Location is needed to request assistance, to match you with a nearby Umusare and, if you choose, to see nearby verified drivers.
  • Before an Umusare accepts a request, they see only an approximate area (about 1 km) and a rounded distance.
  • After acceptance, the driver and the accepted Umusare share exact live locations with each other. This sharing stops when the request is completed or cancelled, and the exact pickup point is then deleted.
  • "Nearby drivers" is optional. If you switch it on, other SafeDrive-verified drivers see only your approximate area (about 1 km), your name, cooperative and group — never your Driver ID, exact position, contact details, vehicle plate or movement history. Switching it off hides you immediately.
  • Nearby drivers are always found around the approximate area SafeDrive last accepted from your device. Location updates are limited in frequency and implausible jumps are rejected, so the feature cannot be used to search other places.
  • Location accuracy depends on your device, and no system can guarantee perfect privacy or accuracy. Do not share your location with SafeDrive if you do not want it used as described here.

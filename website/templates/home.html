SafeDrive AI

An AI-assisted driver sobriety and assistance system. A driver-facing camera and a computer-vision pipeline estimate whether a driver may be affected by alcohol, and help a potentially impaired driver get a verified Umusare (support driver) instead of continuing to drive.

How It Works

Alcohol-related impairment only. SafeDrive AI does not measure blood alcohol concentration and is not a medical or legal test.

1. Driver camera

The camera finds the driver, locates the face and checks that the image is good enough to assess.

2. AI assessment

A lightweight image classifier (MobileNetV3, being developed) scores each frame for alcohol-related visual patterns.

3. Stable decision

A short temporal window combines many frames, so one noisy frame never triggers a warning. The result is Sober, Uncertain or Potentially not sober.

4. Warning and assistance

If the assessment is “potentially not sober”, the driver is warned and can request a nearby verified Umusare (planned).

About SafeDrive AI

A final-year Bachelor of Computer Engineering project by Fabrice Ndayisaba and Mugabo Kevia.

Drivers and Umusare belong to cooperatives, which handle identity, verification and accountability. An Umusare can help only after their cooperative manager has verified them.

Current status: camera monitoring, driver and face detection, accounts and cooperatives work. The alcohol classifier has not been trained or validated yet, so no sobriety results are shown and no accuracy is claimed.

SafeDrive AI Privacy Policy

Version {{ version }} · Effective {{ effective }}

1. Who we are

SafeDrive AI was created and developed by Fabrice NDAYISABA. This policy explains what personal information SafeDrive processes, why, and the choices you have. It should be read with the Terms & Conditions. We aim to handle personal information in line with applicable law, including Rwanda's law on the protection of personal data and privacy.

2. Information we process

  • Account information: name, email address, phone number (optional; used by Umusare for payments), role, password (stored only as a one-way hash), and when you verified your email and accepted these documents.
  • Cooperative information: your cooperative, group, membership status and verification decisions, including any reason a manager gives.
  • Vehicle information (drivers): plate number, and optionally make, model and type.
  • Camera monitoring: while monitoring is on, camera frames are processed to produce an assessment (SOBER, UNCERTAIN, POTENTIALLY NOT SOBER). SafeDrive does not store camera images or face data; technical monitoring records (session times, frame counts, status events) are kept for reliability.
  • Location: see section 4.
  • Assistance and payments: requests, their status and timestamps, journey distance, fares, payment confirmations, ratings and problem reports.
  • Messages: internal messages you send and receive.
  • Security records: an audit log of security-relevant actions (for example sign-ins, verification decisions) with IP address. The audit log never contains passwords, one-time codes, message text or precise locations.

3. Why we use it

To provide and secure the service: creating and verifying accounts, cooperative management, safety monitoring, matching drivers with Umusare, calculating fares, enabling payment confirmation, internal messaging, preventing fraud and abuse, and meeting legal obligations.

4. Location

  • Location is collected only when you use a feature that needs it.
  • For an assistance request, an Umusare sees only an area of about 1 km until they accept. After acceptance, the driver and the accepted Umusare share exact live locations; this stops when the request ends and the exact pickup point is deleted.
  • Only the most recent position is kept while sharing is active — SafeDrive does not keep a location history.
  • Nearby drivers: while you use the nearby map, SafeDrive keeps only your latest approximate area (about 1 km) — never your exact position — to find drivers around you and to reject implausible location jumps. It is deleted after one hour without updates. Other drivers see you only if you switch visibility on (name, cooperative, group and approximate area); switching it off hides you immediately.
  • Cooperative managers and administrators see operational status and approximate areas, not your exact position.

5. Who can see your information

  • Your cooperative manager sees the profile, verification, vehicle and operational information of their own cooperative's members only.
  • Administrators manage the platform and can see account and cooperative information when needed.
  • An Umusare who accepts your request sees your name, phone, vehicle plate and live location for that assistance.
  • A driver you assist sees your SafeDrive identity details and phone number for that assistance.
  • Other verified drivers see only what is described for nearby drivers, and only if you switch it on.
  • Service providers such as email delivery and map-tile providers receive only what they need to provide their service.

SafeDrive does not sell personal information.

6. Retention

We keep information for as long as your account exists and as needed for safety, dispute and security purposes. Live locations and one-time codes are short-lived by design. You may ask for your account to be closed.

7. Security

We use measures such as password hashing, hashed one-time codes, server-side permission checks, CSRF protection and audit logging. No system is perfectly secure; please protect your password and report suspected misuse.

8. Your choices and rights

You can update your phone and vehicle details, switch nearby-driver visibility on or off, and stop location sharing by stopping the feature that uses it. Subject to applicable law, you may ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, or object to certain processing.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. Each version is numbered and dated, and you may be asked to accept an updated version; earlier acceptances remain on record.

# 📧 Quick Start Guide - Send Email to Drivers

## 🎯 In 3 Easy Steps

### Step 1: Open Email Modal
1. Go to your **Admin** or **Chef Dashboard**
2. Click the **"📧 Send Email"** button (top-right corner)

### Step 2: Fill the Form
1. **Select Driver** - Choose from dropdown
2. **Email Auto-Fills** - Your driver's email appears automatically
3. **Add Subject** (optional) - What's the email about?
4. **Write Message** - Your email content here

### Step 3: Send!
1. Click **"🚀 Open in Gmail"**
2. Gmail opens with everything pre-filled
3. Review and click **Send** in Gmail
4. Done! ✅

## 🔍 Troubleshooting

**Q: What if Gmail doesn't open?**
- Make sure Gmail is your default email app
- Check browser permissions
- Try again - might be a timing issue

**Q: Can I see if the driver read the email?**
- No - this uses native Gmail, not logged tracking
- Consider asking driver to reply for confirmation

**Q: Is my message saved?**
- No - messages sent directly via Gmail
- Not stored in SafeDrive_AI database

**Q: Can I send to multiple drivers?**
- Not in this version
- Send individual emails to each driver
